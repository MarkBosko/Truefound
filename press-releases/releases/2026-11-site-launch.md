TrueFoundMovies Launches Independent Streaming Platform for Cryptid, Paranormal and Alien Documentaries

New site offers on-demand rental and purchase access to documentary films mainstream platforms won't carry — no subscription required.

[CITY, STATE] — TrueFoundMovies, a new independent streaming platform, has launched at TrueFoundMovies.com, offering on-demand access to documentary films focused on bigfoot, cryptids, UFO encounters, alien abductions, and paranormal phenomena.

The platform fills a gap left by mainstream streaming services, which rarely carry this category of documentary content. TrueFoundMovies gives viewers instant access to these films without a subscription — titles are available to rent for 48 hours or purchase for permanent access, with a watch link delivered by email.

"These are the documentaries people are actually searching for," said a TrueFoundMovies spokesperson. "Real investigations, credible witnesses, firsthand accounts. We built the platform because this content deserves a dedicated home."

The launch catalog includes films covering some of the most documented cases in cryptid and paranormal research, including multiple films centered on the ongoing investigation of Bigfoot by researcher Tom Biscardi. The platform also features titles examining UFO abduction cases, alien encounters, and unexplained phenomena.

No subscription is required. Viewers pay only for the films they want to watch.

About TrueFoundMovies

TrueFoundMovies is an independent streaming platform dedicated to documentary films about cryptids, the paranormal, and unexplained phenomena. Films are available to rent or purchase instantly at TrueFoundMovies.com.
