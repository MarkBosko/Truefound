TrueFoundMovies Launches TrueFound Vault — Rare Vintage Documentaries Free to Stream, No Ads, No Subscription

A growing collection of groundbreaking films from the 1960s through the 1980s, available to anyone at TrueFoundMovies.com.

[CITY, STATE] — TrueFoundMovies today announced the launch of TrueFound Vault, a curated collection of rare and historically significant documentary films from the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s, available to stream completely free at TrueFoundMovies.com.

TrueFound Vault brings forward a selection of films from an era when independent filmmakers were documenting cryptids, paranormal events, and UFO phenomena decades before the genre became a fixture of cable television. These are original works made in the field, with firsthand witnesses, by people who believed in what they were recording. Many have rarely been seen since their original release.

The collection is available to any viewer without creating an account, entering a credit card, or sitting through advertising. Visitors go to TrueFoundMovies.com and watch. The collection is actively expanding, with new titles added on a rolling basis.

"These films deserve to be seen," said a TrueFoundMovies spokesperson. "They were made by people who went out and documented what they witnessed. We want everyone to have access to them, with no barriers."

About TrueFoundMovies

TrueFoundMovies is an independent streaming platform dedicated to documentary films about cryptids, the paranormal, and unexplained phenomena. In addition to the free TrueFound Vault collection, the platform offers rental and purchase access to new and exclusive documentary releases. Visit TrueFoundMovies.com.
